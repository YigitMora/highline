LINEFLIGHT

Support

Lineflight is a single-player arcade game for iPhone. You cross a city on a line, swinging from building to building without touching the ground. Every line you fire spends rope from a reel that does not refill during a run — so the question is never just can I reach it, but is this one worth the line?

GETTING HELP

Questions, bug reports and feedback go here:

  • github.com/YigitMora/highline/issues — open an issue. A GitHub account is required, and issues are public.

When reporting a problem, the most useful things to include are your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No network access at all — the game works entirely offline.

YOUR DATA

Lineflight collects nothing. Your best run, your progress and your two sound settings stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Hold anywhere to fire a line at the nearest reachable anchor.
  • Let go to release. Release low in the swing to keep speed; release late to trade speed for height.
  • Every attachment spends rope. One long committed swing costs far less than four panicked short grabs.
  • The run ends if you hit a building, hit the ground, or run out of line before the next anchor.
  • Distance earns credit. Credit buys a longer reel, a better winch, and new districts.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
